Space cell technologies
=======================

These examples compare representative space photovoltaic cell technologies
through the public orbitalpv API.

The built-in templates cover five important technology families:

* crystalline silicon;
* single-junction GaAs;
* InGaP/GaAs/Ge triple-junction;
* four-junction III-V;
* inverted metamorphic multijunction (IMM).

The models are intended for system studies, education and early design. They
are not qualification models for a specific manufacturer part.
