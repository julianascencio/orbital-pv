Flying Laptop measurement snapshot
==================================

flying_laptop_right_2018-02-01_03.csv contains 8,599 native calibrated voltage
and current pairs for the right solar array, extracted from packet 4900 of
FLP_TM_HK_2018-02.tar.bz2. Source: FLP Team, DOI 10.18419/darus-451; packet
definitions DOI 10.18419/darus-456, CC BY 4.0. The adjacent provenance JSON
records original and derived SHA-256 values, acquisition interval and selection.

Values are not interpolated, resampled or clipped. Their timestamps are UTC
packet generation times (source documents up to 400 ms acquisition delay).
The subset was selected by time and array, not by prediction performance.
This is measured operating power, not measured maximum-power availability.
