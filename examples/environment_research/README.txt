Environmental data and Earth-view geometry
==========================================

Inspect real NOAA MetOp particle observations and verify a spherical Lambertian
Earth-view integrator against an independent analytic reference. Particle
measurements and modeled thermal forcing retain their distinct units and
measurement boundaries.

See :doc:`/user_guide/modeling_topics/environmental_inputs` for NASA CERES,
POES/MetOp, SPENVIS exports, historical orbital elements and SPICE attitude.
