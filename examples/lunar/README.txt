Lunar surface power and survival
================================

Measured/interpolated LOLA terrain, measured Diviner temperature bins and
computed DE421 Sun/Earth geometry drive five reproducible engineering examples.
Bundled inputs make every example runnable offline; original downloads and
subset provenance can be regenerated with the companion analysis script.

The power, hardware, heater and rover results are model scenarios, not measured
lunar PV output or flight-qualified designs. See the lunar modelling guide.
