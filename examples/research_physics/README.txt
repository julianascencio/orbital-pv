Research physics and power systems
==================================

Explore transient thermal balance, radiation response, spectral current matching,
array circuits and battery-aware compute scheduling through the public
``orbitalpv.physics`` API. These are reproducible screening scenarios with
declared assumptions. Synthetic spectra, circuit faults and mission profiles
are labeled explicitly; they are not flight measurements or exact reproductions
of the motivating research papers.

See :doc:`/user_guide/modeling_topics/physics_research` for equations, input units,
validation limits and executable analyses.
